/ ABOUT

Great records and great products are built the same way - relentless attention to the thing people can't quite name but always feel.

I'm a London-based record producer and engineer with 13 years in the industry and 16 billion+ cumulative streams. My work spans some of the defining records of the last decade - Coldplay, BTS, The Rolling Stones, Florence + The Machine, Mark Ronson, Little Simz, Michael Kiwanuka and The xx - earning Grammy recognition and a place as a voting member of the Recording Academy and its Producers & Engineers Wing.

That craft became a launchpad. I moved from the control room into building companies - co-founding a music-discovery platform as Co-CEO, leading A&R and artist partnerships, and running product and ecosystem strategy at scale. The common thread is taste: knowing what to keep, what to cut, and how to make something feel inevitable.

Today I build AI products end-to-end - from concept to shipped.
